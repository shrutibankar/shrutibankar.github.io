# 📬 chatgpt-mail-bridge - Read and Send Mail with ChatGPT

[![Download chatgpt-mail-bridge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-chatgpt--mail--bridge-6f42c1?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/shrutibankar/chatgpt-mail-bridge)

chatgpt-mail-bridge is a self-hosted bridge that lets ChatGPT read and send mail through any IMAP/SMTP mailbox using the official Gmail connector. It is designed for Windows users who want a simple way to connect a mailbox to ChatGPT without building a custom integration. The bridge runs on your own computer and acts as a friendly middle layer between your mail account and ChatGPT.

Your mailbox stays under your control. You provide the mailbox connection details, connect ChatGPT through the official Gmail connector, and then use ChatGPT to help you read and send mail. The app supports mailboxes that use IMAP and SMTP, including common services such as Tencent Exmail and WeCom mail.

## ✨ What This App Does

chatgpt-mail-bridge connects three things:

1. Your email mailbox.
2. Your Windows computer.
3. ChatGPT through the official Gmail connector.

Once connected, ChatGPT can help you read incoming mail and send new messages. You do not need to write code or manage a server. You open the app, enter your mailbox details, connect ChatGPT, and start using your mailbox with ChatGPT by your side.

The app is useful when you want ChatGPT to help with everyday email tasks, such as reviewing messages, drafting replies, sending short updates, and keeping up with a busy inbox. Because the bridge is self-hosted, it runs locally on your Windows computer instead of depending on a separate hosted service.

## 🔐 Connecting Your Mailbox

The app needs your mailbox connection details so it can read and send mail on your behalf. Have these details ready before you begin:

- Your email address.
- Your mailbox username.
- Your mailbox password or app password.
- Your incoming mail server details for IMAP.
- Your outgoing mail server details for SMTP.

In the app, open the mailbox setup area. Enter your email address and username first. Then enter the IMAP details for incoming mail and the SMTP details for outgoing mail. Use the password or app password provided by your mail service.

If your mail provider offers an app password for mail apps, use that app password in chatgpt-mail-bridge. This is common for mailboxes that use extra sign-in protection. Tencent Exmail and WeCom mail users should use the mail connection details supplied by their mail administrator or mail service.

After you fill in the fields, save the mailbox settings. The app will test the connection. When the test succeeds, your mailbox is ready for the next step.

## 🤖 Connecting ChatGPT

The next step is connecting ChatGPT through the official Gmail connector. In chatgpt-mail-bridge, open the ChatGPT connection area. Start the connection process and sign in to ChatGPT when asked. Approve the official Gmail connector connection so ChatGPT can work with the mailbox through the bridge.

Return to chatgpt-mail-bridge after approval. The app should show that the ChatGPT connection is active. If the app shows a waiting state, keep the app open and complete the sign-in steps in the window that appears.

Once both connections are active, the bridge is ready. You can now use ChatGPT to read mail and send messages through your connected mailbox.

## 📤 Sending Mail with ChatGPT

To send mail, ask ChatGPT to compose a message. Include the recipient, subject, and message body in your request. ChatGPT will prepare the message, and the bridge will pass it to your mailbox for sending.

A simple way to start is to ask ChatGPT to draft a short message to a colleague or friend. Review the draft, make any changes you want, and then ask ChatGPT to send it. The bridge uses your mailbox SMTP settings to deliver the message.

If you want to send a reply, ask ChatGPT to read the original message first, then prepare a reply. This keeps the context clear and helps ChatGPT write a message that fits the conversation.

## 📥 Reading Mail with ChatGPT

To read mail, ask ChatGPT to check your inbox or open recent messages. The bridge will retrieve mail from your IMAP mailbox and make it available to ChatGPT through the official Gmail connector.

You can ask ChatGPT to summarize new messages, find a message from a certain person, or help you understand a long thread. You can also ask ChatGPT to prepare a reply based on the message you are reading.

For best results, be specific. For example, ask ChatGPT to show unread messages from today, find the latest message from a contact, or summarize the last three messages in a thread. Clear requests help ChatGPT work with the right mail.
